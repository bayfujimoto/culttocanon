The advertisement always found my feed. The elusive creature. Back for limited time only. My mood changes in an instant. I have newfound purpose: go to Taco Bell and get the Nacho Fries. A  heretical parody of fast food and Mexican culture. I get them every time. Years of my life pass, punctuated by the seemingly random ebb and flow of the availability of nacho fries. Then, a message from the ether. Poison disguised as a gift. Nacho fries have become a permanent fixture on the menu. My joy is immediate. And fleeting. What's the point? Nacho fries will never be -- and never could have been -- canonized like the Doritos Locos Tacos or the 5-Layer Burrito. And now it has lost its ephemeral nature too. I haven't had it since. It's still there, but I mourn its loss anyways.
