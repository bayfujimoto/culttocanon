Come and gone, come and gone: this was your liturgy, and I kept it. You announced yourself in the feed like an angel with bad news, back for a limited time only, and I went down to the drive-thru as to an altar and received you, heretical wafer, parody of two cuisines. The years were measured in your returns. Then the word came down from the ether, and it was poison in the shape of a gift: you would not leave again. They called it a mercy. It was a death. What is an ode to the imperishable? There is nothing to mourn in what cannot go, and so nothing to love. You were never going to sit among the canonized, the Doritos Locos Taco, the 5-Layer Burrito, the permanent and the praised, and I never wanted you there. I wanted you gone, so that you could come back. Now you stay, and I keep away, and I grieve a thing that is still there.
