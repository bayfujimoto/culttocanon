*Abandoned 2026-04. The argument was thinner than it looked; what remained was an enthusiasm not yet a thesis.*

*Cabinet* (1999–2020) was, for two decades, the strongest argument I knew that a heterogeneous container could hold together — that scholarly essays could sit beside artist projects beside oddities beside fictions without the result feeling miscellaneous. The unifying register was a particular kind of intellectual curiosity; the editorial sensibility supplied the binding agent that no taxonomy could.

What I wanted to write here: an argument that Cult to Canon could learn structurally from Cabinet rather than imitating its content. The thesis didn't survive contact with the actual pieces I wanted to cite. Left in place as evidence of the abandoned.
